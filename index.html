About me

My research focuses on intelligent algorithms for battery management systems, with particular interests in state estimation, remaining useful life prediction, fault diagnosis, and safe fast charging control.

I am especially interested in combining rigorous modeling, data analysis, experimental validation, and reproducible implementation into research that is both technically solid and practically meaningful.
